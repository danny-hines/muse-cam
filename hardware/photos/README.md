# Assembly and completed-camera photos

Danny supplied ten assembly photos and four completed-camera photos for this
build guide. They are stored at their original resolution without cropping or
retouching. Numbering preserves the order within each supplied set: 1–10 for
assembly and 11–14 for the completed camera. The [build guide](../BUILD.md)
embeds the assembly photos beside their steps and includes a
[completed-camera gallery](../BUILD.md#completed-camera).

The photos establish the four-magnet layout: two body magnets plus one in each
door. They also show the display PCB marked `4.3inch DSI LCD`, `800×480 Pixels`,
and `2.2`, and a PiSugar battery marked 5000 mAh / 3.7 V / 18.5 Wh.

The completed views show two enclosure color schemes and optional wrist straps.
They document exterior fit and port access; they do not establish focus quality,
battery runtime, or the result of an application capture test.

Use the [wiring table](../WIRING.md#gpio-connections) for pin numbers; not every
GPIO connection or connector contact is visible. Screw lengths come from the
builder's written notes, not measurements inferred from the images. See the
[remaining checks](../BUILD.md#remaining-measurements-and-checks) for information
still needed to reproduce the build fully.
