# Enclosure concepts

These generated concept renders explore the enclosure form and internal packaging.
They are inspiration only: dimensions, clearances, connector locations, ribbon
routing, mounting points, ventilation, and battery safety must be verified in CAD
and against the physical components before fabrication.

## Concept sheets

- [`muse-cam-enclosure-concepts-v1.png`](muse-cam-enclosure-concepts-v1.png) — three finished exterior directions: friendly retro, rugged technical, and playful modular.
- [`muse-cam-assembly-concept-v1.png`](muse-cam-assembly-concept-v1.png) — an exploded packaging concept plus four illustrative assembly stages.

## Design intent represented

- Rear-mounted 4.3-inch touchscreen and separately mounted front camera module.
- PiSugar mounted in its intended relationship underneath the Raspberry Pi.
- Battery facing a protected, removable exterior service panel.
- Pi processor and passive heatsink facing a ventilated interior plenum.
- Low intake and high exhaust openings without a fan in the first prototype.
- Broad ribbon-cable bends and separation between the camera sensor and hot components.

The artwork may simplify or misrepresent specific board geometry. Use manufacturer
mechanical drawings and measurements from the actual parts for enclosure CAD.
